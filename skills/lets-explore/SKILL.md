---
name: lets-explore
description: Discover knowns and unknowns about a nebulous topic area.
---

This skill is invoked when wanting to research a topic or explore a problem space, that is not ready for building or planning.

First, reach a shared understanding of what we are exploring, and why I am asking it, and why now. Unpack what assumptions are baked into my ask—and verify if they are valid. Ask me questions using /grilling - Check GitHub for related issues & prior research. If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

Once we have a well-defined research question, determine the orthogonal dimensions of the problem space, and use subagents to explore each polar end. Do not take anything in the codebase as a given, the goal is to discover new information, pulling in high quality research for expert sources - documentation, articles, open source code, etc.

Then, present the findings among the knowns, known unknowns, and unknown unknowns. And give recommendations for next steps. Check if this backs up or invalidates any previous decisions or open tickets.

Determine whether to save the findings to a Github issue or local file, or just give me an agent prompt for the next session.
